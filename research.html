Ongoing Research

Drought and heatwave analysis for arid and semi‑arid regions using SPI/SPEI, ERA5‑Land, and station observations.

Overview

I’m currently working under the supervision of Dr. Sanaz Moghim on drought analysis in arid and semi‑arid regions of Iran using the SPEI and SPI indices. We are developing forecasts for 2035 and analyzing the Isfahan drought heatmap for recent years.

Quarter regionalized map of the Zayandehrud River Basin to evaluate each index and identify hotspots.
