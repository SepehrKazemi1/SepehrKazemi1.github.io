PFAS Removal with Advanced Oxidation Processes

International Journal of Environmental Analytical Chemistry, Volume 104 (2024), Issue 20 · DOI: 10.1080/03067319.2023.2225412

Contribution: structured literature review and analysis.

Abstract

Our review compares advanced oxidation processes for removing PFAS from water. We find boron-doped diamond (BDD) electro-oxidation consistently delivers strong removal for long-chain PFAS, and foam-fractionation combined with BDD performs especially well on complex leachates. Photochemical routes are generally limited for stubborn species like PFOS under typical conditions. We outline the underlying mechanisms, typical efficiencies, and when each method is practical given energy and cost.
